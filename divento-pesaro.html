DiVento Pesaro

An interactive installation that tells the Pesaro area through eight multimedia, multisensory stories, all seen from a single point of view: the wind’s.

year
2023
context
Exhibit Design Lab, University of San Marino. Prof. Roberto Paci Dalò, Giorgio Dall’Osso
team
Giulio Mazzanti Dolci, Alessandra Valentini, Sara Seu
exhibited
Storie e Geografie, Galleria Rossini, Pesaro, 7–19 October 2023

context

In 2022 Pesaro was named Italian Capital of Culture 2024, with the motto La Natura della Cultura. The project was one of seven exhibits designed to promote it.

Starting from that motto, the research focused on the two natural landmarks of the area: the sea, and Monte San Bartolo, the hill that surrounds the town.

research

The natural side of the Pesaro area reaches its fullest expression on the slopes of Monte San Bartolo: once at the top, you are surrounded by colours, scents and currents of wind.

Along the way we involved the village of Fiorenzuola di Focara, the villas of Monte San Bartolo, the Assonautica of Pesaro, the Museo della Marineria and the dialect poet Carlo Pagnini.

concept: the wind

The wind is the active side of air, long linked to breath and spirit: in Arabic and Hebrew the same word, ruh, means both. From here came the wish to tell Pesaro, and the character of its land, from the wind’s point of view.

the installation

The space is defined by a suspended metal ring, 12 metres across, with 90 strips of white cotton sewn onto it. The fabric becomes the screen for the stories, and inside, a 360° projection shows the view from the top of Monte San Bartolo.

The circle is divided into eight sections, one for each wind of the compass rose, which is printed on the floor.

how it works

At the centre stands the selector, a weathervane shaped like the sail of a boat, with a ring to grab and turn it. Visitors point the sail towards a wind, and the system projects the matching story.

The experience is not only visual but also tactile: fans behind the structure blow on the fabric where the story is shown, so the panels move and visitors feel a real breeze. The visit is free and has no time limit.

Technology: Arduino, a rotary encoder, eight relays, eight fans and four projectors.

eight winds, eight stories

Each wind carries a story about the area: a dialect poem, the 2017 fire on Monte San Bartolo, the painted sails of fishermen, Dante’s verses about Focara.

  1. Tramontana — Un segrét
  2. Grecale — Fuoco e vento
  3. Levante — Le vele di Pesaro
  4. Scirocco — La Vela
  5. Ostro — Arpe Eoliche
  6. Libeccio — Il Garbin
  7. Ponente — Il Sciò
  8. Maestrale — Dante a Focara

1:1 prototype

A working prototype of the installation, built at full 1:1 scale, with the stories projected on the fabric strips.

identity & print

The name plays on di vento, “of wind”: the italic vento leans like something pushed by the air.

The identity runs across posters, tickets, a leporello with the eight stories, and a book that documents the whole project.

the leporello

A folding booklet collects the eight stories, one panel for each wind.
