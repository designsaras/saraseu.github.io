about

I’m a designer with a background in Industrial Design and Interaction & Experience Design. I enjoy exploring design in all its facets, moving between digital design, UX/UI, graphic design and visual communication.

I’m curious by nature, and I believe every project is a chance to experiment, learn and look at things from new perspectives. I like to start by listening and understanding needs, turning ideas and insights into concrete, functional and carefully crafted solutions.

I believe in dialogue, collaboration and the value of a shared design process, without giving up personal research and the freedom to experiment. I still have a lot to learn, and just as much desire to do it, challenging myself in new contexts and projects.
