about

I studied Interaction & Experience Design at the University of San Marino, after a bachelor’s degree in Industrial Design at the University of Florence. I work with design and digital prototyping tools, and I like working in teams, something that grew through my Erasmus+ in Lisbon and many collaborative projects.

Creative, empathetic and curious about innovation, I am organised, good with people, and able to work on my own in fast, demanding contexts.
